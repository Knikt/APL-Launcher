## 1.1.4
- **Malay (Bahasa Melayu)**, by 9M2PJU (#44): the whole interface, chosen on its own on a screen set to Malay, or in *Settings › General & Privacy › Language*.
- **Android TV: the launcher installs without a Play Protect warning.** Google Play Protect blocked APL Launcher on a Nvidia Shield (*App blocked to protect your device*, only an OK button): it blocks any app installed outside the Play Store that declares an accessibility service. The service for the remote's Home and ⏯ is now in a small second app, **APL Launcher Home Button** (`apl-launcher-home-button-vX.Y.Z-android.apk`, under 1 MB, also under the fixed name `apl-launcher-home-button.apk` for Downloader): only who wants the Home button installs it, with Play Protect's scan turned off for a moment or with adb. The two talk only to each other (a permission Android gives only to apps signed with APL Launcher's key). *Settings › Android TV* and the first starts explain it; APL Launcher Setup installs both. Updated from 1.1.3, Home keeps the TV's own launcher until the module is installed: the launcher says so once.
- **Android TV: the Control Center over apps uses *draw over other apps*** (*Settings › Apps › Special app access*, or *Settings › Android TV › Allow Over Other Apps*; APL Launcher Setup gives it). Without it, holding Home opens the launcher with its Control Center.
- **After a full start, the launcher comes back at once even when Autorun Smart Hub was off.** The screen then showed Samsung TV Plus or the TV input, and the launcher only 30 to 60 s later, when APL Boot started by itself (an S90F, also with Vetro's *Custom launcher*). Autorun Smart Hub is off at such a start when Vetro's *Custom launcher* is on, or when the restart went through a standby (APL Overlay keeps it off then, so that a wake shows no Samsung home). The launcher, which the screen loads behind the TV input about a second after power on (seen on the M8), now starts APL Boot at once, and APL Boot puts it in front. Apps2Samsung package only. Not tried on a screen yet.
- **LG: Guide shows the Control Center over the app** (rooted TV), its video still playing, and Back closes it: no more black screen. Over the launcher, its own Control Center as before. It needs one restart of the TV after the root install.
- **LG: no more freezes on the first pass along the dock.** Measured on a G5: the dock went from 49 % to 85 % of frames on time (its longest frame from 1.4 s to 83 ms), Settings from 68 % to about 90 %, Sources opens in 83 ms instead of 300. The glass panes are made smaller and faster, the small blurs no longer decode a 4K picture, and Settings' white plate slides from row to row as one layer (all TVs).
- **LG: the launcher's own screen saver over the Home Screen**, not LG's: the TV asks its apps first, and the launcher says no while it is in front with its screen saver on. With the screen saver set to Never, or an app in front, LG's runs as before.
- **LG (rooted): the TV no longer opens apps in the background by itself** (its preload list and policy switched off, again every 10 minutes), LG Channels stays closed after a restart too, and Close Unused Apps also works with LG's own Home. APL Launcher Setup switches them off right away, not only after a restart.
- **LG's Top Shelf recommends only titles for all audiences** (Tous publics, U, G, PG, FSK 0/6), nothing unrated.
- **APL Launcher Setup (LG) keeps LG's Home** when it was kept at the last install: running the Setup again never gives Home to APL Launcher without being asked.

## 1.1.3
- **LG: Close Unused Apps**, as on Samsung (rooted TV). An app left 10 minutes in the background closes on its own, and one the TV opened by itself and you never used (LG Channels) after 1 minute: the TV keeps its memory and the launcher stays smooth. Never the app in front, live TV, the inputs, AirPlay, or an app playing sound or video. *Settings › Apps › Close Unused Apps* turns it off. On an LG OLED G5, the swap had grown to 458 MB without it.
- **LG: the apps you don't use never open.** LG preloads LG Channels in the background at every start: its start page is now covered in RAM by a blank one that closes at once (`apl-noload.sh`, the list in `noload.list`). Nothing is written in the system, and one command gives it back. Run the root install once more (APL Launcher Setup, or the one command in the README) to get it.
- **LG: right after an update, the launcher sees root again.** It looked for root once, before `apl-keep-root.sh` had given it back, and hid the root features until it was opened again: it now looks again for a few minutes and reloads once, with your keys and fonts.

## 1.1.2
- **LG: Delete App.** Hold OK on an app, then *Delete App*, as on Samsung: on a rooted TV, for every app LG itself lets you delete (TF1+, M6+, france.tv, Rakuten TV…). LG's system apps (LG Channels, Alexa…) don't offer it. Since Home opens APL Launcher, LG's own menu to delete apps was out of reach. Tried on an LG OLED G5: TF1+ and M6+ deleted in 3 s each.

A watcher started at every boot (`apl-keep-root.sh`) gives root back within seconds, and puts back the files a public package can't have: the SF Pro fonts, your API keys, your avatars. Run the root install once more (APL Launcher Setup, or the one command in the README) to get it.
- **LG: Guide always opens the Control Center**, even when the launcher's service isn't there to catch it: no more black screen.
- **Android TV: Delete App was missing** from the menu when holding OK on an app. It opens Android's own *Uninstall?* question again.
- **LG and Android TV: the launcher says when a new version is out.** They can't update themselves like Samsung: once per version, on the Home Screen, a message gives the new version and how to get it. On LG with the Homebrew Channel: *Open the Homebrew Channel* (then APL Launcher › Update), or *Add the Repository* (its address already typed in) when APL Launcher isn't in its list yet. On LG without it: run APL Launcher Setup again. On Android TV: the Downloader code 9890834, or the Setup. *Check for Update* in Settings asks right away. Tried on an LG OLED G5.
- The Home Screen's hint (*Press ▲ for full screen*) no longer shows through the buttons of a message.
