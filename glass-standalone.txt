================================================================================
CLEANFRAME LIQUID GLASS — SAMART HOME CONTROL CENTRE + SEARCH VIEW
Compiled from the repo's own app (app/main.js, app/style.css, app/catalog.js,
app/settings.js) for hand-off to another app (Tizen TV / Samsung Frame TV).

- All glass here is a pre-rendered, pre-blurred slice of the wallpaper.
  Nothing filters live: every pane/tile/capsule is an <img> or a CSS layer
  that moves by background-position only.
- The Control Center, Home sheet, folders and the status capsule all share
  one glass model: window.CleanFrameGlass = { src, lens, offset } in main.js.
- Search sits over the same live backdrop (stage-scale blurred poster tile, or
  the prepared photo-wallpaper field), so the sheet frosts over the real
  backdrop without any live filter on the TV.
================================================================================
